The preview form

The contact form checks required fields and the format of an email address in your browser. It has no delivery destination. It does not send an enquiry, create an appointment or save your entries to a database.

Values remain in the current page’s memory while it is open. This website does not write them to cookies, local storage or session storage. Your browser may independently retain form values or page history according to its own settings. Use fictional details and avoid sensitive information.

Tracking and storage

This preview includes no analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, embedded media, file uploads, payments or booking tools. All website assets are local. The application does not set cookies or store personal information.

Local server requests

When viewed through a local HTTP server, your browser requests page and asset files from that server. The server may log request paths, timestamps and connection information depending on its configuration. Form values are never included in those requests by this website.

Service links include only a service category in the URL to preselect the contact form. That category may appear in browser history or local server logs. It does not include your name, email or message.

Details needed before launch

The client must supply the firm’s legal identity, applicable jurisdiction, privacy contact, approved enquiry delivery process, hosting arrangements, retention periods and any relevant data processors.

The live notice must explain the purposes and applicable legal basis for processing, recipients, rights, security arrangements and complaint routes under the applicable law. These details have not been supplied or approved.

A preview, not a live firm notice

No live enquiry service is configured. The client must review and approve the final privacy notice and data handling before publication.
